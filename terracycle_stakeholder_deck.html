Why the raw +0.11 was misleading

Selectors — engaged that week
99%
Nearly every selector either swapped a recipe or bought another HFM add-on. The remaining 1% clicked the envelope itself — still engagement.
Non-selectors — engaged that week
81%
About one in five non-selectors never engaged with the menu that week.
The mechanic
To add the envelope, a customer had to actively engage with the HelloFresh menu that week. We define "engaged" as: swapped a recipe (in or out) OR bought another HFM add-on. Engaged customers order more in the following weeks regardless of any envelope, so the raw comparison was mostly showing "engaged customers order more than non engaged ones." Once we compare selectors to other engaged customers, and adjust for how much they already order (loyalty band) and box size and other add-ons in the same week, the effect drops to +0.016 orders / 5W (p ≈ 0.56).

How this was measured

Population
All CA customer-weeks in the 8 program weeks (W03-W05, W11-W15). n = 6,687 selectors, 724,657 non-selectors.
Outcome
5W AOR: orders in the next 5 weeks (0-5 scale). Source: aor_next_5_weeks_in_percent / 100 × 5.
Three comparisons
Raw (vs all non-sel), Intermediate (vs engaged non-sel only, no other adjustments), Adjusted (engaged non-sel pool, then stratified on loyalty × box shape × other-add-on-this-week).
Loyalty
customer_activation_loyalty_bucket_mealboxes, coarsened to 12 bands: 1 / 2-5 / 6-10 / 11-15 / … / 46-50 / 51+.
Engagement definition
"Engaged with HFM that week" = recipe swap (in or out) OR any other HFM add-on. Selecting the envelope is itself an act of engagement, so all selectors are treated as engaged. Used as a filter on the non-selector arm.
Result
Raw +0.112 (SE 0.027). Intermediate −0.012 (SE 0.027, p ≈ 0.66). Adjusted +0.016 (SE 0.027, p ≈ 0.56). No statistically significant envelope effect on retention.
